How the standings work

Teams are ranked by regular season wins. When teams finish level on wins, the criteria below break the tie, in order. A step only matters if every step before it ended level.

    The playoffs

    • The top six make it. Seeds 1 and 2 get a bye in the first round.
    • Quarterfinals: seeds 3–6. The league has run these both ways: by seeding (3 vs 6, 4 vs 5), and with seed 3 picking its opponent.
    • Semifinals: seeds 1 and 2 play the quarterfinal winners, either by seeding (seed 1 gets the lowest seed left) or with seed 1 picking.
    • The semifinal winners play for the title. The losers play for third.
    • Once a season ends, places 1–4 come from those games. The two quarterfinal losers are 5th and 6th, ordered by the same tiebreakers. Places 7–12 stay in regular season order.
